Car branding in Dhaka

Car branding in Dhaka can turn an everyday business vehicle into a recognisable part of your identity. A delivery car, a service fleet and a campaign vehicle need different levels of information. Some need a clear name and contact route. Others need a strong campaign visual. Dhaka AD helps you explore car wraps, fleet graphics and relevant vehicle-advertising options. We start with the vehicle and the job it does. Then we shape the design, production and installation around the practical surfaces available.

01

Decide what the vehicle needs to say

Is the aim to make your team easier to recognise, introduce a product or point customers towards a service? Pick the main purpose first. A simple identity treatment can be more useful than an elaborate campaign on a vehicle that spends its day handling deliveries or service calls.

  • Car-wrap and partial-wrap options.
  • Consistent fleet graphics across different vehicle models.
  • Campaign vehicles and LED covered-van options to discuss separately.

02

Keep the design consistent across the fleet

Different doors, panels and windows mean one flat design rarely fits every vehicle unchanged. Keep the brand system consistent while adapting the layout to each model. Review the contact details carefully. A printing error on one file can become a very mobile mistake across the fleet.

03

Plan around business operations

Vehicles may need time out of service for installation. Discuss surface preparation, fitting, care instructions and removal before fixing the schedule. For hired campaign vehicles, confirm where and how they will operate, and what evidence is included. Vehicle branding is a physical production job as well as a design task.

04

Make recognition useful

If someone spots your vehicle and searches the business name later, they should find a clear, current way to reach you. Match the name, contact details and offer across your vehicle, website and social profiles. Familiarity is valuable when the next step is easy to take.

05

A simpler way to start

Tell us who you want to reach, where and when. Bring any existing artwork. We will discuss suitable locations, creative requirements and reporting, then confirm the scope in a written proposal you can review.
