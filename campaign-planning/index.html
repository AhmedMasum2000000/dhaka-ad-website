Outdoor campaign planning in Bangladesh

A clear campaign starts with a few useful decisions. Who needs to notice? Where will they see you? What should they do next? Dhaka AD helps you connect those answers with real locations, suitable formats and a workable schedule. Use this guide to prepare your brief, then call to turn the shortlist into a plan.

Questions worth asking

How do I check a location for my campaign dates?+

Call with the location name, intended start date and campaign length. The team will check the placement and the current arrangements, then help you review the next steps before booking.

Is a screen’s operating schedule the same as my airtime?+

Operating hours describe when the screen runs. Your campaign schedule describes when and how often your creative appears within that window. Confirm the daily allocation, breaks and booking calendar for the selected screen.

Can I start with one location?+

Yes. A focused location can be the right starting point when it fits the audience and objective. Ask us to compare that placement with a small, relevant group.
