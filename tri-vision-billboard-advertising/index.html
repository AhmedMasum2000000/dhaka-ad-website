01 / HOW IT WORKS

Three messages, one premium position

Triangular slats rotate together so the board changes face every few seconds. You can tell a short story across three faces, show three products, or share the board with two other advertisers and hold one face for less.

02 / WHERE

Tri-vision locations in Dhaka

  • Tennis Club, Shahbagh: facing Motsho Bhaban.
  • Paribagh: facing Bangla Motor.
  • Jibon Bima Tower, Stadium Gate 3: facing Motijheel and Dainik Bangla.

03 / THE CREATIVE

Write for the turn

Each face is seen for a few seconds before it rotates. Make every face complete on its own, then make them stronger together: a teaser, a reveal, a call to action.

What is a tri-vision billboard?+

A rotating billboard whose triangular slats turn together to show three different printed faces in sequence. The movement attracts attention that a static board cannot.
