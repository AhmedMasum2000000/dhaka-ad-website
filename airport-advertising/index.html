Airport advertising in Dhaka

Airport advertising in Dhaka can put your brand around journeys that begin or end at the airport. The exact location matters. An Airport Road billboard, an entrance-facing screen and a railway-station placement offer different views and contexts. Dhaka AD helps you compare the available formats against the audience you want to reach. Whether you are promoting a travel service, a hotel or another relevant offer, start with a clear purpose. We will help you review the location, creative requirements and campaign conditions before you book.

01

Be specific about where the ad will appear

The word airport covers a broad set of surroundings. Ask whether a proposal means Airport Road, an entrance-facing location, the railway-station gate or a separately approved terminal placement. Those descriptions are not interchangeable. A clear photograph, address and facing make the discussion much more useful.

  • Airport entrance-facing LED options.
  • Airport Railway Station entry-gate advertising.
  • Roadside placements along the airport approach.

02

Write for someone with a journey on their mind

Travellers and the people meeting them have practical things to do. Make the benefit easy to understand. A simple destination, recognisable service or useful offer usually gives the design a clearer purpose than a collection of brand claims. Adapt the language to the audience rather than assuming everyone shares the same needs.

03

Agree the placement and the practical details

Confirm the surface, campaign dates, artwork specifications and required approvals in writing. Access restrictions, production steps and the selected media format can affect the schedule. For LED, ask about daily airtime and delivery reporting. For physical branding, agree installation and photo-reporting arrangements.

04

Make the next contact feel easy

The message on the placement and the information at the next touchpoint should agree. If a traveller calls or visits your website, the offer, availability and contact route need to make sense. An attractive ad can open a conversation; a useful response helps keep it going.

05

A simpler way to start

Tell us who you want to reach, where and when. If you have a rough budget range, bring that too. We will discuss suitable options and confirm the scope in a written proposal. You can review the plan before making a decision.
