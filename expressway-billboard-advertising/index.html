01 / THE PROBLEM

Street-level ads get lost in Dhaka's clutter

At a busy junction your board competes with shop signs, banners, wires and traffic. On an expressway the view opens up: fewer competing messages, long sight lines and drivers looking ahead, not down.

That is why the brands that need scale fast, property developers, telecoms, banks, automotive and FMCG launches, fight for expressway faces first.

02 / WHERE

Three expressway corridors, one call

  • Dhaka Elevated Expressway: Chairman Bari crossing (125' × 40', lit) and Banani–Kakoli (two stacked faces). See Banani & Mohakhali
  • Purbachal 300 Feet: Jolshiri Bus Stand (two 100' × 30' faces), Kuril Bishwa Road, Kanchan Bridge and the Jolshiri entry. See Badda & Purbachal
  • Dhaka–Mawa–Bhanga Expressway: Abdullahpur and Rajendrapur in Keraniganj, both directions toward the Padma Bridge and Dhaka. See Keraniganj

03 / THE CREATIVE

Design for 60 km/h

Expressway viewers have two or three seconds. Brand big, one striking image, five words or fewer. A phone number rarely survives the speed; a memorable name or web address does.

  • One idea per face, not three.
  • High contrast colours that hold up in haze and dusk.
  • Ask for a mock-up on the real photograph before printing.

04 / THE PROCESS
