Explore the IDB–Mirpur corridor

The route reference lists 20 pieces from IDB Bhaban to Mirpur-10 and seven at Mirpur-12. Treat this as the proposed route inventory. Confirm the exact current positions, number of usable panels and display dimensions before production.

02

Give repetition a clear purpose

Use the same recognisable identity across the route. One concise message can be easier to follow than a different idea on every panel. If the design changes along the sequence, keep the brand and next step consistent.

03

Plan the physical work

Agree the panel surfaces, two-sided artwork, reflective material, printing, installation and maintenance responsibilities. Review the final dimensions and permissions for each selected position.
