Bus branding in Dhaka

Bus branding in Dhaka lets your campaign travel with an agreed vehicle or route. It can give a brand a distinctive physical presence without relying on one fixed roadside view. Yet a bus wrap is more than a poster with wheels. Doors, windows, panel shapes and real operating conditions all affect the design. Dhaka AD helps you explore full wraps, side panels and back panels against your campaign brief. Start with the areas you want to cover, then confirm the vehicles and production scope in writing.

01

Ask about the vehicle and the route

Understand which vehicles are included and how their routes are described. Proposed coverage is not a promise of the same journey every day. Discuss operating changes, maintenance and how you will be informed if an agreed vehicle becomes unavailable. A useful plan explains the practical limits as clearly as the visual idea.

  • Full-wrap designs where the vehicle and permissions allow.
  • Side-panel branding for a focused visual message.
  • Back-panel options with clear artwork dimensions.

02

Design around a working bus

Keep important text away from panel joins, door movement and other obstructions. Think about the side view and the rear view separately. Each may need a different composition while keeping the same message. Approve the artwork on a vehicle-specific layout before anything is printed.

03

Agree production and care

A wrap has to be produced, fitted and eventually removed. The proposal should explain the materials, installation access, campaign period and responsibilities for damage or replacement. Ask how the branding will be checked during the campaign. These details are less glamorous than a mock-up and just as useful.

04

Make the message easy to carry away

A moving vehicle gives the audience a brief encounter. Choose one offer or one memorable brand idea. If a phone number or website is essential, check its readability in the real layout. Keep the long explanation for the landing page, where the reader has chosen to stay.

05

A simpler way to start

Tell us who you want to reach, where and when. Bring any existing artwork. We will discuss suitable locations, creative requirements and reporting, then confirm the scope in a written proposal you can review.
