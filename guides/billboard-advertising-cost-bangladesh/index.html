How much does billboard advertising cost in Bangladesh?

Every advertiser asks the same first question. Most rate lists online are out of date before they are published. Billboard rates in Dhaka move with the season, the site and demand, so a figure from last quarter can mislead you.

This guide explains how each format is priced, what pushes the cost up or down and which extras to expect, so when you ask for a quotation you know exactly what you are looking at. For today's actual rates, call us: it takes minutes.

02 / WHAT DRIVES COST

Seven things that move the price

  1. Position: Gulshan, Banani and expressway faces command more than residential streets.
  2. Size: larger faces cost more, though not in a straight line.
  3. Facing: the side that meets heavier or slower traffic is worth more.
  4. Term: longer bookings usually lower the effective monthly cost.
  5. Season: Eid, Pohela Boishakh, year-end and election periods push demand up.
  6. Illumination: neon, lit and LED formats cost more than printed boards.
  7. Package: combining several faces or formats often unlocks a better rate.

03 / THE FINE PRINT

Costs that are often left out

  • Printing of the billboard skin is normally separate from the rent.
  • Installation and any re-installation if you change artwork mid-term.
  • VAT, tax and AIT are usually quoted on top of the media rate.
  • Foreign remittance tax may apply if payment comes from abroad.
  • Content production for LED and metro screens.

Our quotations show media, printing, installation and taxes as separate lines, so nothing appears later.

04 / PAYMENT

How payment usually works

Typical structures are an advance with the work order and the balance staged across the term for static boards, 100% advance for 30-day metro campaigns, and an advance plus balance after installation for yearly airport signs. Approved artwork is due about 72 hours before a static board goes up, and about five days before a metro campaign airs.

05 / GET MORE FOR LESS

How to get the best rate

  1. Book early for Eid and year-end.
  2. Be flexible on the exact face: a nearby alternative can be cheaper and just as visible.
  3. Combine formats: a static board plus an LED screen on the same route.
  4. Take a longer term where you can.
  5. Ask us to compare three options side by side.

How is LED billboard advertising priced in Bangladesh?+

LED billboards are sold as airtime: a cost per minute per screen, for a minimum number of minutes each day (often 60), across the dates you book. Premium junctions cost more per minute than secondary locations.
