LED billboard vs static billboard: which should you choose?

Choose wrong and you pay for a year of a message that should have run for a fortnight, or a fortnight of a message that needed a year.

LED screens sell time; static boards sell space. Here is how they differ, and when each one wins.

QUICK ANSWER

Choose an LED billboard for short campaigns, motion, several messages or fast changes; you buy minutes of airtime per day. Choose a static billboard for a long-running, always-on presence on one route; you rent the face, usually for a year, and print one design. Many brands combine both on the same route.

02 / CHOOSE LED WHEN

LED wins for speed and story

  • You are launching something with a date: a sale, an event, a release.
  • You want motion, or several messages in one campaign.
  • You need to start fast or change creative mid-campaign.
  • You want a premium junction for weeks, not a year.

03 / CHOOSE STATIC WHEN

Static wins for permanence

  • You want to own a route all day, every day.
  • Your brand is building long-term familiarity.
  • You need a giant face that LED cannot match.
  • You want to point people to a nearby outlet or project.

Questions buyers ask before they call

Is an LED billboard better than a static billboard?+

Neither is better in general. LED suits short, time-bound or changing messages; static suits long, always-on presence. Choose by goal and term, or combine both on the same route.

Is LED billboard advertising cheaper than static?+

For short campaigns LED is often cheaper because you pay only for the airtime and days you need. For a year-long presence, a static board usually costs less than equivalent LED airtime.
