THE 7 STEPS

The process at a glance

  1. Define your audience, preferred areas and campaign dates.
  2. Get a shortlist of available faces with photographs, sizes and facing.
  3. Compare a written quotation with media, printing, installation and taxes as separate lines.
  4. Approve a mock-up of your artwork on the real location photograph.
  5. Confirm the work order and payment schedule.
  6. Supply final artwork about 72 hours before installation (five days for metro screens).
  7. Receive installation photographs or the LED log summary as proof.

01 / PREPARE

What to have ready before you call

  • Who you want to reach and what you want them to do.
  • The areas or routes that matter: near an outlet, a project, a university.
  • Your start date and how long you want to run.
  • Any existing artwork or brand guidelines.

02 / AFTER YOU BOOK

What happens next

We print, install and send photographs for static boards, or schedule your file and send the log summary for LED screens. If something needs attention during the term, you have one number to call.
