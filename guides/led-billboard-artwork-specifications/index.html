LED billboard artwork specifications: get your file right first time

A file in the wrong shape gets stretched, cropped or rejected, and your campaign starts late. Here are the specifications we see most often, and the checks that save you a reprint or a re-edit.

02 / RULES

Creative rules that survive the street

  • Brand visible in the first second and the last.
  • One message per spot, five to seven words on screen at once.
  • Big, high-contrast type; nothing thinner than a bold sans.
  • No essential information in sound; most screens are silent.
  • Leave a safe margin around the edges.

03 / DEADLINES

When to send files

  • LED billboards: ahead of the start date, after we confirm the specification.
  • Metro train screens: approved content about five days before airing.
  • Static billboards: approved artwork about 72 hours before installation.
