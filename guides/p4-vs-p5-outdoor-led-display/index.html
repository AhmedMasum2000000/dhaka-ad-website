P4 vs P5 outdoor LED display: which one should you install?

Buy the wrong pixel pitch and you either overpay for detail nobody can see, or install a screen that looks blocky up close. The number after the P is the gap between pixels in millimetres. Smaller means sharper, and more expensive.

P4 has 4 mm between pixels and P5 has 5 mm. P4 looks sharper at close range and costs more per square foot; P5 costs less and is ideal when viewers are further away. A rough rule: the minimum comfortable viewing distance in metres is about the pitch in millimetres. Both should be fully outdoor, around 6,000 cd/m² bright and IP65 weatherproof.

02 / SPECIFICATION

What a good outdoor screen should have

  • Fully outdoor rating with IP65 weather protection.
  • Brightness around 6,000 cd/m² for daylight.
  • 120° horizontal and vertical viewing angle.
  • High refresh rate so it films without flicker.
  • A rated lifespan around 100,000 hours.
  • Controller software for USB, app and cloud playback.

Questions buyers ask before they call

What is the difference between P4 and P5 LED screens?+

P4 has a 4 mm pixel pitch and P5 a 5 mm pitch. P4 is sharper up close and costs more; P5 is more economical and looks excellent from further away.

Which LED screen is best for outdoor advertising in Bangladesh?+

For roadside billboards viewed from a distance, P5 (or larger) usually gives the best value. For shopfronts and close viewing, P4 is worth the extra cost. Both must be IP65 and bright enough for daylight.
