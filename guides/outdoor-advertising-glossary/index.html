A–Z

25 outdoor advertising terms, explained simply

OOH (out-of-home) advertising
Any advertising people see outside their homes: billboards, LED screens, transit, airport and street furniture.
DOOH (digital out-of-home)
Out-of-home advertising shown on digital screens, such as LED billboards and metro train screens.
Static billboard
A printed advertising face that shows one design for the booked term, usually rented by the year in Dhaka.
LED billboard
An outdoor digital screen that shows rotating video or still adverts; airtime is sold per minute per day.
Airtime
The amount of time your advert appears on a digital screen, usually measured in minutes per day.
Loop / rotation
The repeating sequence of adverts on a shared LED screen.
Log summary
A report from a digital screen showing when your advert played, used as proof of delivery.
Facing
The direction a billboard faces, and so which stream of traffic sees it.
Unipole
A billboard mounted on a single tall pole, common beside highways.
Tri-vision
A billboard made of rotating triangular slats that shows three faces in turn.
Neon / lit billboard
A board with built-in illumination so it remains visible after dark.
Mid-island branding
Advertising panels mounted on road medians, usually repeated along a stretch of road.
Reflective sticker
Printed retro-reflective material that lights up in vehicle headlights.
Light box
An illuminated frame displaying translucent, backlit printed film.
Billboard skin
The printed PVC or flex sheet stretched over a billboard frame.
Pixel pitch (P4, P5)
The distance in millimetres between LED pixels; smaller pitch means a sharper image at close range.
Nits (cd/m²)
A measure of screen brightness; outdoor LED screens need several thousand nits for daylight.
IP65
A weather-protection rating meaning dust-tight and protected against water jets.
Mock-up
A preview of your artwork placed on a photograph of the real location.
Work order
The client's written confirmation of a booking, usually issued with the advance payment.
AIT
Advance income tax, often added to advertising invoices in Bangladesh.
Reach
The number of different people who see a campaign at least once.
Frequency
How many times, on average, each person sees the campaign.
Brand activation
A live, physical experience that lets people interact with a brand, such as a launch, sampling or roadshow.
Newspaper insert
A printed leaflet placed inside newspapers before delivery.
