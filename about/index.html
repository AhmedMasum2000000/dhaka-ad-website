01

An idea needs a place to work

Your brief gives the campaign its purpose. The locations and channels give it context. We bring the two into one discussion so the creative, production and media decisions support the same goal. You can ask about one service or compare a connected mix.

02

See the proposal before you commit

Location photographs and format mock-ups make the conversation more concrete. The quotation should identify the placements, deliverables and practical conditions. You should understand what you are approving, which details still need confirmation and what happens after approval.

03

Agree what proof looks like

For LED campaigns, discuss log summary reporting. For physical installations, agree the location and installation photos. For digital campaigns, decide which platform measures and business outcomes can be checked. Reporting is useful when its purpose and limits are understood.

04

A conversation with a useful starting point

Bring your intended audience, preferred area and dates. Add your current artwork if you have it. If you are still exploring, say so. We can help you compare suitable formats and work out the next step.
