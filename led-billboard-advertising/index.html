LED billboard advertising in Dhaka

LED billboard advertising in Dhaka gives your message a place on the streets your customers use. The useful question is not simply how big a screen looks. It is whether the people passing it are the people you want to reach. A launch near Dhanmondi needs a different shortlist from a campaign built around Mohakhali commutes. Dhaka AD helps you compare real locations, review your artwork and plan airtime around your campaign. Start with an area and a date. We will help you turn that rough idea into a proposal you can actually assess.

01

Choose a screen for the journey, not just the photograph

An impressive photograph can start a conversation. It should not finish the decision. Look at the screen's facing, the route beside it and the distance from your shop or service area. A placement can look excellent and still sit on the wrong side of your customer's day.

  • Landmark placements for a clear focal point.
  • Several locations when your audience moves between areas.
  • A local shortlist when enquiries need to stay close to your business.

02

Make one message easy to catch

Your audience has a city to navigate. Give them one reason to look, one thing to remember and one next step. A launch date, a clear offer or a recognisable product can do more work than a small essay on a very large screen. We review the artwork against the selected placement before scheduling.

03

Understand what you are buying

Ask how many minutes per day are included, how they are distributed and which dates the campaign covers. Operating hours describe the screen's schedule; they do not mean your ad owns every minute. Screen dimensions, video dimensions, breaks and booking minimums also need checking for the exact location.

  • A named location and screen facing.
  • Agreed dates, daily airtime and delivery requirements.
  • A written plan with the placement, production and reporting clearly identified.

04

Review the plan, then check the delivery

You should be able to recognise the placement in your proposal and understand what happens next. Dhaka AD uses location photographs and creative previews before approval. After an LED campaign, log summary reporting helps you review delivery. Agree the reporting dates and included information before the campaign begins.

05

A simpler way to start

Tell us who you want to reach, where and when. Bring any existing artwork. We will discuss suitable locations, creative requirements and reporting, then confirm the scope in a written proposal you can review.
