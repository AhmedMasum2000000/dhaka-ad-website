P4 & P5 outdoor LED installation in Bangladesh

Outdoor LED display installation in Bangladesh brings your own display project into focus. A roadside location, retail frontage or building façade needs more than a screen specification. It needs a suitable structure, power arrangements, clear content and a practical handover. Dhaka AD helps you discuss P4 and P5 display options, supporting fabrication, fitting and fixing as one project. Start with the site and the distance from which people will view it. 01

Start with the site

Share a photograph, the intended screen position and approximate dimensions. Review the viewing distance, exposure to weather, supporting surface and service access. A site assessment should guide the final design.

  • Site dimensions and orientation.
  • Viewing distance and intended content.
  • Power, structure and maintenance access.

02

Compare P4 and P5 for the actual viewing distance

P4 and P5 are display options to discuss for the project. Review the viewing distance, cabinet layout, brightness, weather protection and controller for the site. Confirm the exact model, physical dimensions, controller and final technical specification in the approved project schedule.

03

Bring the screen and structure together

Define the display, supporting fabrication, fitting, fixing, transport and electrical responsibilities in one agreed scope. Include the structure and weather protection in the site discussion. Confirm the final construction and installation method before work starts.

04

A handover your team can use

Agree commissioning checks, controller access, content loading, user guidance and the aftercare process. Confirm the warranty scope, exclusions and support contact in writing. The project is easier to run when your team knows how to update the content and who to call for help.

05

A simpler way to start

Tell us who you want to reach, where and when. Bring any existing artwork. We will discuss suitable locations, creative requirements and reporting, then confirm the scope in a written proposal you can review.
