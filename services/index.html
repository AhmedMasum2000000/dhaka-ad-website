02

Choose the channel around the goal

For a physical destination, start with the journeys around it. For an online enquiry, look at the ad, landing page and follow-up together. For a launch, consider which part of the experience introduces, explains or demonstrates the offer. A useful plan explains the role of every channel.

03

Keep one version of the offer

A connected campaign does not mean copying one layout everywhere. It means keeping the central message, brand details and next step consistent, while adapting the creative to each setting. Agree who approves the work and how changes are shared.

04

You stay involved in the decision

We discuss the brief, prepare suitable options and explain the scope. You review the locations, creative and quotation before approval. The final plan sets the deliverables and reporting. Call with an audience, area and date; those three details give the conversation a useful starting point.
