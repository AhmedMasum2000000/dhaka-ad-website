Billboard advertising in Dhaka

Billboard advertising in Dhaka gives a brand a physical place on a chosen road. It is useful when you want people to connect a name, a product or a destination with an area. But a large surface cannot rescue a small idea. The message still needs to make sense at a glance. Dhaka AD helps you explore static billboard, unipole and hoarding options, review the location and agree production details. Bring your preferred route and campaign dates. We will help you weigh presence, practicality and execution together.

01

Choose the road before choosing the artwork

Start with the journey that matters to the business. Is the placement close to a shop, along a commute or part of a broader launch? Check the direction of travel, visibility and surroundings. A site visit or a clear set of recent photographs can answer questions a location name cannot.

  • Static billboards for an established message.
  • Unipole options where the format suits the route.
  • Hoardings and other approved large-format surfaces.

02

Use the space with discipline

Large-format advertising is an editing exercise. Decide what a person should remember if they catch only the headline and brand. Keep the core visual clear. If contact details matter, check whether they can be read from the intended distance. More space is not an instruction to add more words.

03

Separate media, production and installation

Ask for a quotation that explains the placement period, printing or other production, installation and any finishing or removal work. Confirm the artwork dimensions with the production team. A physical board's dimensions and the final print file should never be guessed from a photograph.

  • Location photographs and placement identification.
  • Final artwork approval before production.
  • An installation schedule and photo reporting.

04

Give the campaign a useful next step

A nearby landmark or a simple destination can help interested people find you later. If the message sends people online, make the destination easy to remember and useful on a phone. Keep the promise consistent between the billboard, the landing page and the person answering the enquiry.

05

A simpler way to start

Tell us who you want to reach, where and when. Bring any existing artwork. We will discuss suitable locations, creative requirements and reporting, then confirm the scope in a written proposal you can review.

Questions worth asking

Is a static billboard better than an LED screen?+

They serve different needs. Static boards keep one design on a physical surface for the agreed period. LED bookings use scheduled airtime and can support motion. Compare the audience, location, creative needs and execution requirements.
