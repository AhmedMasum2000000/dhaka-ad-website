LED and large-format settings

Use the photographs to talk about visibility, composition and the relationship between a screen and the street. Ask for a current photograph of the exact placement you are considering. A gallery image is a reference, not a live booking confirmation.

02

A mock-up and a delivery report do different jobs

A mock-up helps you assess the design before it runs. A delivery report helps you review the agreed work afterwards. Keep them clearly labelled. Neither a photograph nor a preview, on its own, proves how many people noticed an ad or became customers.

EvidenceWhat it helps you check
Placement photographThe documented location and visible surroundings
Creative mock-upHow the proposed artwork fits the selected format
Installation photo reportThe documented installed work and location
LED log summaryThe delivery information included in the agreed report
Platform reportingThe digital measures recorded for the agreed campaign

03

What a useful case study would need

For a measured results story, ask for the objective, dates, scope, baseline, measurement method and supported outcome. Client permission also matters. We do not fill missing records with invented testimonials, awards or sales figures.

Questions worth asking

Can I see my ad on the location before booking?+

Ask for a creative mock-up using the selected location photograph or format layout. It helps you review message size, composition and the placement context. A mock-up is a preview; it is not evidence that a campaign has already run.

What proof will I receive after my campaign?+

For LED campaigns, discuss the log summary report and its delivery schedule. For installations, agree photo reporting that identifies the placement and work. Digital campaigns use the relevant platform reports. Decide what evidence is included before approving the plan.
