LED billboard price in Bangladesh: compare the full plan

LED billboard price in Bangladesh becomes easier to understand once you know what the quotation covers. The number beside a location is only part of the decision. Daily airtime, campaign dates, package contents and creative production all matter. This guide helps you compare the plan before comparing the total, so you can ask better questions on the call.

01

What actually changes the price?

LED billboard price in Bangladesh depends on what you are buying, where it runs and for how long. A quote for one screen and a short daily allocation cannot be compared fairly with a package of several locations. Start by making the scope visible. Then decide whether that scope fits the campaign.

  • Location and facing: the actual placement, not only the neighbourhood.
  • Daily airtime: the agreed advertising minutes or delivery basis.
  • Campaign dates and length: the complete booking calendar.
  • Package contents: one screen, multiple screens or a defined group.
  • Creative work: design, adaptation or video production if required.
  • Other agreed charges: production, installation and applicable taxes.

02

Operating hours are not your advertising minutes

A screen may operate across a broad daily window. That does not mean one advertiser receives that whole window. Your proposal should state the airtime you are buying and how it is distributed. Ask about breaks, scheduled closures and the dates on which the allocation applies. If two offers use different delivery units, ask the providers to explain them in the same terms before you compare the prices.

03

A useful quote is easier to compare

Put the placement name, dates and delivery basis beside the amount. Then ask what is included and what is separate. A price can appear lower because it excludes creative production, uses fewer locations or covers a shorter period. None of those arrangements is automatically wrong. The problem is discovering the difference after approval.

05

A calculation you can use without guessing a rate

Where the provider quotes a rate per advertising minute, the basic media calculation is: agreed rate per minute × booked minutes per day × booked days. For several placements, calculate each separately unless the quote specifies a package rate. This is a planning formula, not a published Dhaka AD price. Apply discounts only when they appear in the written offer, and add the other agreed items separately.

06

Why a smaller shortlist can be a better starting point

If the budget is limited, begin with the most relevant audience and route. Ask what one useful placement would achieve within the campaign, then compare it with a small group. A larger screen or a longer list of areas is not automatically a better business decision. You should be able to explain why each item belongs in the plan.

07

Keep the artwork budget in the conversation

An existing video may need adapting to a particular screen or campaign message. A static layout may need motion work, different dimensions or a simpler headline. Ask for that review early. It is easier to decide the media plan when you understand whether the required creative is ready, included or a separate piece of work.

08

Agree the booking conditions before paying

The quotation and work order should explain payment milestones, quote validity, artwork deadlines, changes, cancellation and the handling of interruptions. These conditions can vary between offers and suppliers. This website does not replace them with a universal policy. Keep the approved placement names and creative versions in the same record as the financial terms.

09

What should you send for a relevant quotation?

Send your brand, objective, intended audience, preferred locations, dates and budget range. Say whether you have approved artwork or need production support. If you are comparing an existing proposal, share its scope rather than asking only whether the total can be beaten. A clear brief makes the discussion faster and the answer more useful.

  • Your goal: a launch, a local enquiry, a store visit or broader recognition.
  • Your preferred area and dates, with any flexibility.
  • The daily airtime or campaign scope you want to compare.
  • The creative work already available and the support you need.

Is a screen operating schedule the same as my booked airtime?+

No. Operating hours describe when a screen can run. Your booking describes the minutes or other agreed delivery your ad receives within that schedule. Confirm breaks, the placement of those minutes and the campaign dates in your proposal.

Can I advertise with a smaller budget?+

Share the budget range and the outcome you want. Ask which location, format or campaign scope is practical. A focused plan may be more useful than stretching the same budget across unrelated channels. There is no universal minimum quoted on this website.
