02 / THE AUDIENCE

Who will see your brand here?

Industrial Dhaka. Amin Bazar, Baipail, Dour Moor and Dhamrai sit on the routes that serve the garment belt, the EPZ and the highway north-west. Hundreds of thousands of workers, managers and travellers pass these junctions, so mass-market brands get scale fast.

03 / THE CREATIVE

Make this board impossible to ignore

A printed static board carries one design for the whole term, so it rewards a single, confident idea: one image, your brand, five to seven words, and something people can act on later.

  • Ask us for a mock-up of your artwork on this exact photograph before you print.
  • We confirm the final print size and file format with production, never from a photograph.
  • Approved artwork is needed about 72 hours before installation.
