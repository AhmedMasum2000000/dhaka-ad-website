02 / THE AUDIENCE

Who will see your brand here?

The road to the Padma Bridge. Since the expressway opened, Keraniganj has become the front door for traffic between Dhaka and the southern districts. Boards facing Dhaka and the bridge catch long-distance buses, trucks and private cars at highway scale.

Typical audience: Long-distance travellers, southern-district commuters, freight and bus traffic.

03 / THE CREATIVE

Make this board impossible to ignore

Neon and lit boards work twice: by day as a large-format board and after dark as one of the few bright things on the road. Keep shapes simple so the lighting reads cleanly at night.

  • Ask us for a mock-up of your artwork on this exact photograph before you print.
  • We confirm the final print size and file format with production, never from a photograph.
  • Approved artwork is needed about 72 hours before installation.
