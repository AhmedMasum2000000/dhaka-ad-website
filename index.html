Budget & booking

How much does LED billboard advertising cost in Bangladesh?+

There is no single price for every LED placement. The selected location, daily advertising minutes, dates, campaign length and number of placements affect the quotation. Creative work and applicable taxes should be identified separately. Ask for a written quote for your brief.

Read the pricing guide ↗Talk through your campaign · 01840 860 284 ↗
Is a screen operating schedule the same as my booked airtime?+

No. Operating hours describe when a screen can run. Your booking describes the minutes or other agreed delivery your ad receives within that schedule. Confirm breaks, the placement of those minutes and the campaign dates in your proposal.

Can I advertise with a smaller budget?+

Share the budget range and the outcome you want. Ask which location, format or campaign scope is practical. A focused plan may be more useful than stretching the same budget across unrelated channels. There is no universal minimum quoted on this website.

Should I choose an LED screen or a static billboard?+

LED supports scheduled digital creative and motion. A static billboard holds one design on a physical surface for the agreed placement period. Compare the location, viewing distance, production requirements, campaign goal and total cost before deciding.
