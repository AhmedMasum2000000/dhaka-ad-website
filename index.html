01 / THE PROBLEM

WHY MOST OUTDOOR ADS DISAPPEAR

Dhaka sees thousands of ads a day.
It remembers a handful.

Your customer's commute is a wall of noise: shop signs, banners, screens and traffic. Most brands pay to become part of that wall. A few are placed where the eye actually lands, on the right road, facing the right way, at the right size. That difference is not luck. It is planning.

01

The wrong side of the road

A great board facing away from your customers is just expensive scenery. Direction, height and the speed of traffic decide whether anyone reads it.

02

03

No photographs, no screen log, no proof. You are left hoping your money was well spent.

Costs & rates

How much does billboard advertising cost in Bangladesh?+

It depends on the format. Static billboards are usually rented by the year, priced by position and size. LED billboards are sold per minute of daily airtime. Metro screens are priced per minute for 30 days. Printing, installation, VAT and AIT are usually extra. Call for today's rates on specific sites.

How is LED billboard advertising priced?+

As airtime: a cost per minute per screen, for a minimum number of minutes per day (often 60), across the dates you book. Premium junctions cost more per minute.
