01

Three things make the call more useful

Tell us your audience, area and dates. If you have a budget range, creative files or an existing proposal, mention those too.

  • Audience: who needs to notice or respond?
  • Area: which city, neighbourhood, route or business destination matters?
  • Dates: when does the campaign need to run?

02

Ask for a plan you can review

The next step may be a location shortlist, a format comparison or a written quotation. Ask what information is needed, what the proposed scope includes and which details still need checking. Booking availability and delivery conditions belong in the written agreement.
