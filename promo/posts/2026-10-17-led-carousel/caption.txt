Booking an LED billboard in Dhaka? Check these 7 things before you pay 👇

1. You're buying airtime, not a screen. Ask how many brands share the loop and how long your spot runs.
2. One message, one image, your logo. People pass at road speed.
3. Design for midday sun: big, bright, high-contrast.
4. A screen at a slow signal beats a bigger one on a fast road.
5. Re-cut your TV ad: no sound on the street.
6. Agree how you'll see proof it played.
7. Pick the street your customers use, then the screen.
