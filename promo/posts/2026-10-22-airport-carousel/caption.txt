Thinking about an airport ad? Ask these 5 questions before you book ✈️

1. Where exactly will it appear? Arrivals, departures and the airport road reach people at very different moments.
2. Who passes it? A traveller at the luggage belt is not the same audience as a driver on the airport road.
3. How long do they have? People waiting at a belt can read more than people walking past.
4. What do you want them to do next? Make the next step one easy action.
5. How will you see it running? Agree the proof before you pay.
