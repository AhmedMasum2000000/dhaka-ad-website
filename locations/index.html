A useful location checklist

Look beyond the name. Check the view from the intended approach, the physical dimensions, orientation and the next step your customer should remember.

  • The exact placement, facing and recent photographs.
  • Whether it is one screen or a multi-screen package.
  • Your campaign dates, daily airtime, breaks and any closure days.
  • Creative requirements, approvals and the reporting included.
