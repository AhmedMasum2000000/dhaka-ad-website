03

Check this before you finalise the artwork

Two screens around the same circle can face different traffic. Ask which direction your message will be seen from. Confirm the dimensions, orientation, file format, daily advertising allocation and booking dates in the written proposal. Recent photographs and a mock-up help you review the idea in context.

04

Bring the local plan together

Keep the message and contact details consistent wherever the campaign appears. If a person in Gulshan looks up your business later, the page, offer and person answering the enquiry should tell the same story. Ask whether another channel has a useful role, rather than adding it by default.
