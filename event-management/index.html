01 / THE RISK

One weak supplier can sink the night

Separate vendors for the stage, screens, lights and sound means four schedules, four crews and nobody owning the moment when something goes wrong at 8pm. Guests do not see vendors. They see your brand.

02 / WHAT WE BUILD

Everything the stage needs

  • Stage and truss: platforms, ground support, lighting rigs and backdrop frames.
  • LED video walls: modular screens sized to the stage. LED screen rental
  • Lighting: moving heads, beams, washes and effects programmed to the show.
  • Sound: PA, monitors and engineers.
  • Live camera and control: multi-camera capture, vision mixing and screen feeds.
  • Branding: printed backdrops, stage skirts, signage and photo walls.

03 / THE PROCESS

How a show comes together

  1. Brief: date, venue, guests, run of show.
  2. Design: stage layout, screen size, lighting plan.
  3. Quote: one clear budget for the whole production.
  4. Build: our crew installs and tests on site.
  5. Show: our technicians run it live.
  6. Strike: we clear the venue on schedule.
